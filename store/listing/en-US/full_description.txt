CarBook is the player for your own audiobooks: pick a folder, press play and always continue from your exact spot, on your phone and in the car with Android Auto.

No account, no subscription, no ads. Your files stay yours and stay on your device.

🔹 Pick up where you left off
Every book remembers its track and exact position. Resume even by pressing play on your headphones or your car's Bluetooth, without opening the app.

🔹 Automatic library
Pick a folder: each subfolder becomes a book, in the right order even for multi-disc sets. You can also import single files, grouped by album. New folders are added to the ones you already have.

🔹 Organized your way
Group books by author and see what's in progress and what you've finished. Covers are read from the book's folder.

🔹 Chapters
CarBook recognizes chapters embedded in MP3 (ID3) and M4B/M4A files and lets you jump between them.

🔹 Listening speed
From 0.8x to 2x with one tap. Your chosen speed is remembered.

🔹 Sleep timer
From 5 to 60 minutes or at the end of the track, with a gentle fade: fall asleep and the audio stops on its own.

🔹 Skips that fit you
Choose how far to skip back and forward: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds.

🔹 Android Auto
Browse your library on the car screen with the Continue listening, Recent and All books categories, search by voice, and use dedicated controls for skips, chapters, speed and timer.

🔹 Privacy
No sign-up, no data collected by the developer, no tracking. CarBook only accesses the folders and files you choose.

🔹 Formats
MP3, M4A, M4B, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, AAC and WAV.

🔹 Languages
Italian, English, Spanish, French and German.

Note: CarBook does not sell or include audiobooks and does not play DRM-protected files. It plays the audio files you already own.
